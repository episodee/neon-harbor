[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/episodee/neon-harbor/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An original sun-soaked harbor crime adventure. Play Rio, a driver newly arrived in Neon Harbor, and take four risky jobs from fixer Mara Voss. Steal cars, explore the waterfront, outrun patrols, and take back the harbor ledger.

WASD move/drive · mouse look/aim · Shift sprint/boost · Space jump on foot, brake/handbrake in a car · E enter/exit/steal · F or left mouse shoot · right mouse punch · R reload · M mute · P/Esc pause · Enter start/accept. Reach the amber marker to progress. Pause to save; Continue restores your save. Camera motion, shake and flashing can be reduced in the pause menu.

## Run it

`node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.

`mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`

The game works offline with local Three.js and synthesized WebAudio. No build step or remote assets.
